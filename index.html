ABOUT THE PRODUCTION

ICEY Archive is the official companion website for the fictional psychological horror film ICEY.

Every character, event, location, organization, recording, interview, newspaper, document, photograph, and piece of evidence presented on this website is entirely fictional.

The documentary style, archive interface, and "found footage" aesthetics are storytelling devices created exclusively to support the narrative of the film.

Nothing on this website represents real people, real missing-person cases, real government agencies, or real historical events.
